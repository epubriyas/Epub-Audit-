# Page spec — `/industries`

## Why this needs editing

`Content support shaped around your industry.` sounds polished but does not identify the service categories covered. This is an **industry selection hub**, not a single service page. The H1 should communicate both the service subject and the page's by-industry structure.

## Search/user intent
- **Primary:** commercial investigation and audience navigation.
- **Audience:** publishing/scholarly communications; education; technology/documentation; corporate communications; media; accessibility-related content organizations.
- **Primary action:** find the industry, follow relevant service links, request a quote when ready.
- **Keyword theme:** digital publishing and localization services by industry. This is a qualitative candidate, not a verified high-volume keyword.

## Exact required edits
1. Replace hero H1 and intro exactly as in `03_PROPOSED_COPY.md`.
2. Change process H2 `A clear process for connected content.` → `How We Assess Industry-Specific Content Projects`.
3. Change FAQ H2 `Practical answers before you begin.` → `Frequently Asked Questions About Project Requirements`.
4. Set proposed meta title and description from `03_PROPOSED_COPY.md` after verifying metadata implementation.

## Must preserve
- Six existing industry cards, their exact audience/industry headings and their order unless a separate ticket approves restructuring:
  1. Publishing & Scholarly Communications
  2. Education & eLearning
  3. Technology & Digital Content
  4. Corporate & Business Communications
  5. Media & Entertainment
  6. Accessibility & Inclusive Content
- Existing card-specific source materials and linked services.
- Existing `Explore industries` anchor behavior, project quote CTA, FAQ content and workflow.
- Do not imply all services or language pairs are actively supported or automatically included.
- Do not add dedicated industry landing pages under this heading-only ticket.

## Content/SEO safety
- Do not make the page a near-duplicate of `/services`.
- Keep headings about **industry use cases**; direct service detail belongs on existing service URLs.
- SEO title should be descriptive but should not repeat all six industries or insert a location keyword.
- If current content is locale-dependent, mark translation strings for review rather than fabricating translations.

## Acceptance
- H1 describes the role of the industry directory.
- All six cards and their service links remain visible and functional.
- Navigation does not change and no new canonical is created.
- Hero and long heading fit mobile viewports without clipped text.
