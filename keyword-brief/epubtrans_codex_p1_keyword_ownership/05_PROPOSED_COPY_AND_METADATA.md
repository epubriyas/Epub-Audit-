## `/data-conversion-services`

**Recommended H1 (only if current wording is ambiguous):** PDF-to-Word & Document Conversion Services  
**Primary theme:** PDF-to-Word, OCR and editable document conversion  
**SEO title candidate:** PDF-to-Word & Document Conversion Services | EPUBTRANS  
**Meta description candidate:** Convert suitable PDFs and scanned documents to editable Word files or agreed formats. Explore OCR, PDF recreation and file-preparation services.

**Hero / opening:**
> EPUBTRANS supports publishers and localization service providers with PDF-to-Word conversion, OCR and document recreation. We assess source quality, target editability and layout requirements before defining the appropriate conversion workflow.

**On-page H2 anchors / directory-card entry:**
- `PDF-to-Word Conversion & OCR` → `id="pdf-to-word-ocr"`
- `PDF Recreation & Editable Document Preparation` → `id="pdf-recreation"`

**Decision help:**
> Choose the first pathway when extracting or editing content is the priority. Choose document recreation when maintaining a closely comparable page layout and rebuilding editable elements is important. A single project may require both.

**Must preserve:** scanned source limitations, tables/equations/figures, difference between editable and visually similar files, input/output requirements, QA boundaries, CTA.
