# SEO and claim guardrails

## Indexation / URLs
- Preserve the observed 16 distinct service paths; do not change slugs for keyword matching.
- Two `/services` directory links can resolve to one canonical URL with different fragments. Fragments are **not separate indexable pages** or separate canonical targets.
- Do not create synthetic duplicate conversion pages for each service card.
- Do not set JATS page's canonical to SciELO or reverse. They serve distinct tasks; use their own production canonicals when indexable.
- No redirects, rewrites, noindex, robots, sitemap edits, or URL consolidation without explicit review. If such a change becomes essential, prepare a separate migration proposal with 301 map and evidence.
- Preserve trailing-slash and preferred-domain conventions actually configured in production; do not assume File 03 examples are live.

## On-page SEO
- One primary intent and one visible H1 per service page.
- Different service pages should have differentiated titles, primary intro, H2s and buyer guidance; avoid "all publishing services" generic introductions on every page.
- Secondary terms should naturally support owner pages without exact-match repetition.
- Do not erase useful technical details simply because they share a term with another page.
- Keep metadata unique. Google may display a different snippet; no guaranteed pixel lengths.
- Descriptive internal anchors to actual live/implemented targets; keep essential content crawlable/server-rendered.
- Preserve structured data only if accurate and aligned with visible service scope. Do not duplicate FAQ schema or fabricate ratings/awards.
- Maintain helpful backlinks/user journeys where possible by preserving existing paths.

## Business and factual guardrails
- Position EPUBTRANS as a **digital publishing and content transformation production partner**, not primarily as a translation agency.
- Verify software versions, supported output formats, languages, media types and specialist standards before publishing.
- SciELO provider listing, even if publicly observed, does not establish that all services/versions/outputs are certified; management must approve exact public wording.
- Do not invent validation guarantees, error-free conversions, platform acceptance, turnaround, staff size, metrics, logos, testimonials or case studies.
- Keep a high-quality enquiry path with the required project inputs, rather than writing repetitive SEO prose.

## Review gate before a new page, merge or redirect
A separate approval is required, supported by:
1. verified canonical URL inventory and status codes;
2. GSC query/page, clicks/impressions and organic landing-page trends if available;
3. conversion/lead quality, current service fit, backlink/referral risk;
4. SERP intent and substantive content distinction;
5. proposed redirect/canonical/internal-link/sitemap plan if URLs truly change.
None of these metrics is provided in this package.
