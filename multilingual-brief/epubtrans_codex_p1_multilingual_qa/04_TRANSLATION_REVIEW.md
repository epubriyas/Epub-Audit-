# Translation and Terminology Quality Gate

## What must actually be translated
- Page title, meta description, H1, intro, service capabilities, inputs/outputs, process, QA, caveats, FAQs and CTAs.
- Navigation, services directory, audience paths, footer, breadcrumbs, cookie/privacy notices as applicable.
- Quote/contact form labels, options, validation errors, confirmations and accessible announcements.
- Informative alt text/ARIA names and social metadata if displayed in the locale.

A translated menu on an otherwise English page is `UI_ONLY`, not an indexable full translation.

## EPUBTRANS B2B terminology safeguards
- Position EPUBTRANS as a digital publishing and content-transformation production partner, not primarily a translation agency.
- Keep distinctions: publisher/academic publisher vs localization service provider (LSP)/translation agency.
- Preserve `JATS XML`, `SciELO`, `EPUB`, `PDF`, `OCR` and other format/tool names where appropriate.
- Preserve distinctions between book/journal typesetting, multilingual DTP, post-translation DTP, translation, copyediting/proofreading, accessibility remediation and XML conversion.
- Preserve scope qualifiers (agreed specifications, availability, subject to source review, not guaranteed compliance).
- Do not invent certifications, turnaround times, language-pair counts, testimonials, client names or claims.

## Approval workflow
For each translated page, an appropriate human reviewer must confirm linguistic quality, service meaning, CTA intent and form terms. Record reviewer and date; unreviewed locales are `BLOCKED` for SEO release. Avoid automatic publishing of raw machine translation.

## Mobile/script review
- French/Spanish/German: expansion, accents and menu wrapping.
- Hindi/Tamil/Telugu/Bengali: glyph shaping, font coverage and line breaks.
- Chinese (Simplified)/Japanese: punctuation and responsive wrapping.
- Arabic: bidirectional text, directionality, form fields and mixed English technical terms.

Where complete translation does not exist, retain English and provide honest UX; don't emit a thin indexed localized page.
